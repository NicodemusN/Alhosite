Info 1

Alhonmaa is a great nation. Exquisite, Expansive and Delusive.

Parliament

The parliament of Alhonmaa is called “The Great Council” in English. The real Finnish name is suuri neuvosto. The parliament has the right to accept or dismiss laws represented by its representatives.

King

King acts like a chairman of the parliament, helping it to achieve solutions in ruling the nation. He is also the formal head of the state and he can declare martial law. King’s authority is further discussed in the constitution.

Since 31st of August 2021, Alhonmaa has had no King. The Council has ruled in its place. There was a motion to elect a king in July 2025, but this has been postponed to December.
